FAIRAXIS WEBSITE — NETLIFY DEPLOYMENT

Files:
- index.html: full responsive website
- thank-you.html: form confirmation page

Deploy to the existing Netlify site:
1. Extract FAIRAXIS_High_Engagement_Website.zip on your computer.
2. Open your existing Netlify site dashboard.
3. Open Deploys.
4. Drag the extracted folder (containing index.html and thank-you.html) into the manual deploy area, or upload the files as supported by the dashboard.
5. Wait for the deploy to finish, then open your existing site URL.

Important form note:
The enquiry form is configured using Netlify Forms markup (data-netlify="true"). After deploying, check the site's Forms area in Netlify. If form detection was not enabled/detected for this deploy, enable form detection as offered in the dashboard and redeploy. Test with a non-sensitive sample enquiry. Form submission is not guaranteed until tested on the live site.
