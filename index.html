Frequently asked questions

Useful to know before you reach out.

Have a different question? Send it through the contact form and outline your requirement.

What kinds of opportunities can I discuss with FAIRAXIS?

Commercial real estate, industrial properties and warehousing, engineering and EPC-related projects, subcontracting, machinery and equipment sourcing, chemical procurement, and B2B partnerships.

Does FAIRAXIS guarantee a property, supplier, project or deal?

No. Enquiries are reviewed on a requirement-led basis. Availability, supplier capability, authority, specifications, pricing, compliance and any proposed commercial arrangement must be independently verified.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Share your organisation or role, opportunity type, location, scope or specifications, approximate quantity or area, target timeline, budget range if available, and the best way to contact you.

Can suppliers, owners and contractors submit opportunities?

Yes. Include the details of what you represent, service or delivery coverage, relevant specifications, availability, and confirmation that you are authorised to discuss the opportunity.

Does FAIRAXIS operate internationally?

FAIRAXIS welcomes enquiries involving India and international markets. Cross-border opportunities depend on feasibility, applicable regulations, documentation, counterparties and verification.
